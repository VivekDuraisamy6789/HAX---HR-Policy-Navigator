# United Kingdom leave guide

> Synthetic training document. Not official Sourcegraph policy. Figures marked as policy facts match the real handbook.

## Paid parental leave in United Kingdom
Eligible teammates in United Kingdom receive 100% of salary for up to 16 weeks per birth, and up to 16 weeks for one or multiple adoption or placement events per 12 months. Where a country or state benefit exists, Sourcegraph covers the difference.

## How to start
Complete the leave questionnaire. Your Leave Case Manager will reach out in 1-2 business days with a leave plan. You may take the weeks consecutively or non-consecutively within 12 months of your first leave date.
