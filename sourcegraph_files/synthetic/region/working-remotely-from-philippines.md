## Remote work in Philippines
Working from Philippines as a regular location requires that payroll, tax and employer-of-record arrangements exist for that country. Do not begin working from a new country for an extended period until the People Team confirms. Short trips are fine for most teammates but still require manager awareness.

Equipment is shipped within 3 business days of approval. Home office items follow the expense rules: receipts over $25, Airbase submission.
