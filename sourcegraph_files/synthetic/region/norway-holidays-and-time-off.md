# Norway holidays and time off

> Synthetic training document. Not official Sourcegraph policy. Figures marked as policy facts match the real handbook.

## Time off in Norway
Teammates in Norway follow the company time-off policy and the published holiday calendar. Request time off in advance, tell your manager, and mark it on your team calendar. Local public holidays that differ from the company calendar are confirmed with the People Team.

Leave longer than two weeks is handled as a leave of absence; your manager and the Leave Case Manager coordinate coverage.
