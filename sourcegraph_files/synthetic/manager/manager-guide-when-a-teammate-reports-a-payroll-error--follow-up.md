# Manager guide: when a teammate reports a payroll error - follow-up

> Synthetic training document. Not official Sourcegraph policy. Figures marked as policy facts match the real handbook.

## Follow-up when a teammate reports a payroll error
Respond to the teammate within 48 hours. Keep the conversation confidential, listen first, and involve the People Team when policy, leave, pay or legal questions arise. Do not promise outcomes that depend on policy you cannot confirm.

For leave over two weeks, direct the teammate to the leave process. For learning requests, the $1000 per year budget applies. Outside work requires review under the outside employment policy.
