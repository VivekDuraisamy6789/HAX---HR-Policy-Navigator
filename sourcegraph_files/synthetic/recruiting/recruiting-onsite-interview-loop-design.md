## Onsite interview loop for design roles
Interviewers submit a scorecard within 24 hours. Candidates receive a decision or next step promptly. Offers require approval before being extended, and candidates have 5 business days to respond. Benefits discussed with candidates must match the benefits page; premiums at 100% apply to US-based full-time employees.
