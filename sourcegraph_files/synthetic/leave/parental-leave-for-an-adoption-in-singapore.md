## Scenario: an adoption (Singapore)
Eligible teammates receive 100% of salary for up to 16 weeks, within 12 months of the qualifying event. State or country benefits are supplemented so the total reaches full pay.

Notify your manager and People Team early; the Leave Case Manager contacts you within 1-2 business days of your completed questionnaire. For adoption cases, share the supporting date (birth, adoption or placement) when requested.
