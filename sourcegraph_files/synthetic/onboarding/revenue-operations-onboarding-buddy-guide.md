## Being a buddy for a new Revenue Operations teammate
Buddies are paired for 8 weeks. Meet at least weekly, answer questions about tools and norms, and introduce the new teammate to people across Revenue Operations. Buddies do not replace the manager and are not responsible for performance feedback.
