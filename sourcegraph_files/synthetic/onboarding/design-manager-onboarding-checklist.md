## Manager checklist for a new Design hire
- Before day 1: confirm equipment is ordered (shipped within 3 business days), accounts requested, buddy picked.
- Week 1: hold a kickoff, share goals for the 30-day check-in.
- Hold check-ins at day 30, 60 and 90 and record notes.
