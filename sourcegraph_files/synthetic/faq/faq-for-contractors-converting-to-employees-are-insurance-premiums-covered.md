# FAQ for contractors converting to employees: Are insurance premiums covered?

> Synthetic training document. Not official Sourcegraph policy. Figures marked as policy facts match the real handbook.

## Are insurance premiums covered?

Asked often by contractors converting to employees.

**Answer:** Yes, 100% of medical, dental and vision premiums for US-based full-time employees and dependents.

If your situation is unusual, ask the People Team before acting.
